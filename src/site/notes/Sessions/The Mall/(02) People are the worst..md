# Summary

## Chapter 6: Angry Mob

Back at the mall entrance, Maggie is still waiting by the doors with the group she has gathered. For now, they stare at the wall of flesh outside, hoping someone daring enough will test the barrier and figure out whether an exit is possible.

Then somebody coughs. It's a teenager, Robbie, and as soon as he does, the people around him take steps back, forming a circle. Bruce, the maintenance man, had also been coughing before something burst out of his chest.

Maggie calls for calm and makes Robbie a proposition: they can't trust that he doesn't have something inside him, so why not prove his commitment by testing the barrier? If he walks out, the rest will follow, and everyone will be safe. Robbie refuses and breaks into a run, but Maggie sends two of her followers after him. They grab him, drag him to the barrier, and push him through.

When the group, Gayle, Casper, Zeke, and Newt, returns, they see Robbie's legs disappearing into the barrier. A moment later, they see his blood pooling at the base of it. Gayle calls Maggie what she is: a murderer. Maggie insists he was one of "them", and the witnesses around her back her up. They have to. To say otherwise would be to admit the blood is on their hands too.

The group moves to pull away, believing it best to avoid the crowd, but Casper has other ideas. He walks up to Maggie, draws his knife, and slashes at her, barely missing, and tracing a thin pink scar across her cheek and nose, knocking her red Gucci glasses from her face. Two men tackle him immediately and start beating him into the ground.

Zeke throws a handful of firecrackers to scatter the crowd, and he and Newt chase after Casper before he can go back for Maggie. As they flee, Gayle fires a few shots of her pistol into the air to break up the mob.

They then reach the basement, and Gayle wedges her mop through the door handle behind them, hoping to keep the angry mob on the other side.

## Chapter 7: Doggie

Less than an hour ago, the group discovered bodies in the vents, each one fruiting what appeared to be spores. They shut down the A/C across the entire mall to stop the circulation.

On the way there, a creature had burst from the vents above their heads and dropped onto Newt. Zeke sliced it open with his boxcutter and it retreated, but in that moment they had nearly lost their friend.

Heading back up, they went looking for Logan, the DMV Express employee. Earlier they had found strange symbols drawn all over his locker and hoped he might be a lead. Unfortunately, they didn't see him at the DMV.

Instead, Casper followed a fresh trail of blood to Polly's Pet Emporium, and near the entrance, he spotted a clerk laying mangled on the floor, barely recognizable. Further in, where it was dark, the caged animals cried in panic. Casper moved toward the sound.

Something large was waiting in the back, and Casper decided to call out to it.

Suddenly, a beast the size of a tiger that looked like a cluster of dogs fused into a single mass launched itself from the shadows onto the shelves he was hiding behind. Casper bolted, and Newt pulled the gate shut behind him as soon as he made it out, right before the thing could get out. The gate wasn't going to hold long, so the group retreated into Sam Goody and locked the door.

For the next fifteen minutes, they listened. Screams. Ten, twenty, maybe thirty voices, all cut short one by one as the beast moved through the crowd. When it was over, a third of the people trapped in the mall were dead. They knew it was safe to leave when the screaming had stopped.

## Chapter 8: There's Something on Your Face

An Orange Julius would hit the spot. they all agreed. Newt led them to the counter and served everyone a drink. The carpet of bodies was visible if they turned around, so they looked the other way and sipped their smoothies.

That's when Newt noticed Zeke's skin: red, patchy, spreading across his face and arms. They traced it back to earlier, when Zeke sliced the creature open and caught a spray of fluid. They agreed he should rinse it off.

Then Casper found a mud trail leading into the vents, wide enough to fit through. Mud had been turning up all over the mall: a note from Bruce even suggested it had been seeping in from odd places for a while. Casper wanted to follow it. The group pushed back, but he wouldn't let it go, so Gayle decided to go with him. Newt handed them one of the walkie-talkies he had found in the lockers, and the group split.

Newt and Zeke headed upstairs to find a bathroom to wash off whatever was on Zeke's skin. but on the second floor, they found Maggie mid-speech. She had made her mind that the group had unleashed the beast deliberately to thin out the survivors, proof, she said, that Casper, Zeke, Newt, and Gayle had already been turned. Newt and Zeke slipped back before they were spotted.

Back downstairs, the situation was no better. The beast was still there, circling the center stage and the Oldsmobile. There was nowhere left to go.

## Chapter 9: Cream of Mushroom

Crawling through the vents, Casper and Gayle followed the mud trail. Then Casper noticed a body wedged inside one of the narrow side-ducts branching off the main passage. It was soft, boneless, almost, and it was moving toward him, pushing itself through the tight space.

He shouted at Gayle to move and pushed forward, but she couldn't maneuver quickly enough through the cramped vent. Behind him, he heard the duct burst. The soft thing forced itself into the larger passage, grabbed him by the ankle, and began pulling him in, absorbing him, and dissolving the boundary between itself and him. Gayle smashed through the floor of the vent and the three of them dropped through.

Once on the ground, they fought it off. Gayle used her last bullet to pull it off Casper. When it was over, they looked around and understood where they were: the mines beneath the mall. That explained the mud.

They found a crack in the wall leading into a bunker, recently used and still stocked with gear and rations. Casper cracked open a can of Cream of Mushroom, and a few bites in, a vision hit him: Mr. Neil, the mall manager, naked in a pit of writhing flesh, moaning.

He shared this daydream with the group, but his friends agreed he probably should have kept it to himself.

Not long after, they realized smoke began seeping into the vents, pushing them out of the bunker and into the dark of the mines, with only Gayle's flashlight and the hope that another way out existed somewhere in the dark.
