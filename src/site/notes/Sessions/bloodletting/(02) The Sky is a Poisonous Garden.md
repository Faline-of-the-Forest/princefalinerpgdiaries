# Summary

## **Chapter 6: The Fever You Hold on This Night**
The Embrace is different for each kindred, but for Wendy, it came like a fever breaking — deathly cold. Reggie had to tell his guest Andrew what he had done, but the headhunter, having lost so much to the Traditions, cared little for their breach. He knew, however, that an illegal Embrace could bring trouble to himself as well, so he warned Reggie to be careful or he would clean up his mess personally.

After a day of rest, Wendy woke hungry. She still could not bring herself to acknowledge what she had become, and Reggie did not have the heart to tell her. In her confusion, she had concluded that Reggie must be an angel: she had felt herself dying and then, impossibly, brought back to life. Reggie asked his housemate to keep watch over her while he drove to Blood Haven to fetch bags.

The drive was quick, though Reggie had the nagging feeling he was being watched. Back home, he fed Wendy what he had brought enough to last her a week or more. Using Dominate, he made her believe she was drinking nothing more than juice. He watched as she leaped from the bed like an animal and tore hungrily into the bags, making a mess of herself. He stayed by her side and said nothing.

## **Chapter 7: The Moon is Full and the Stars are Bright**
Amélie had been promised a meeting with Baron Cimitiere, a man she had so far been careful to avoid, much as she avoided Kindred politics in general for the sake of her own peace of mind. Lidia came to collect her early in the evening and led her to him. She had Amélie blindfolded to ensure she would not learn the location, and brought her to the Baron's hounfour.

They found him smoking a cigar and studying his own ley line maps. He had Amélie sit with him and spoke at length about how much he had respected her grandmother, and what a shame it was that she had rejected the Embrace. They held different opinions, he said, on how the Kindred condition intersected with their faith.

Amélie asked that they focus on his research, having little interest in revisiting his nostalgia. The Baron obliged. He explained that he needed older maps of the ley lines in order to triangulate information from centuries past. With the right data, he said, it was possible to estimate the position of the lines at any point in history, including the period before Kindred first arrived in New Orleans.

Baron Cimitiere seemed eager to share what he knew, which visibly upset Lidia. She protested his willingness to teach Amélie knowledge she herself had brought to him from the Ordo Dracul. When their session concluded, the Baron identified a point of interest on the map and marked it along Bayou St. John. He invited Amélie to join him the following night to investigate it. She still had no clear sense of what drove his interest in the ley lines, but she agreed, curious enough to find out.

## **Chapter 8: A World Full of Poison and Hate**
After Reggie returned to their haven and relieved Andrew of his watch over the newly Embraced Wendy, the headhunter set out to track down Caitlin Meadows, the target given to him by the Prince. Since she had last been seen in the French Quarter, he decided to begin his search there.

His first stop was Antoine Savoy, who had reportedly come close to Final Death at Meadows' hands. In conversation, however, Savoy found the idea that Meadows was responsible to be almost laughable. He had not seen his attacker's face at all, he said, as the figure had been wearing a mirrored mask. Though Savoy played along and allowed that it might have been Meadows, Andrew came away with the distinct impression that the man was withholding something, and that what Vidal had told him might not be entirely true.

Andrew lingered in the club after their conversation, keeping Savoy in his periphery. For an hour he sat and listened, his heightened senses trained on the man's discussions across the room. At some point, a mortal woman entered the club and walked directly to Savoy. She told him:

"Corrigan went to Blood Haven, but he returned home. He hasn't left since."

Savoy thanked her and asked her to keep him informed if that changed. She nodded and left.
## **Chapter 9: And He Cried "Nevermore"**
Elvys was kept chained beneath Perdido House for a full day before Donovan came to collect him and bring him before the Prince. The audience was brief.

Vidal made no effort to conceal his contempt, both for Elvys as a Nosferatu and as a neonate Embraced without permission. His very existence, the Prince said, was a crime against God. But when Elvys revealed that it was the Kindred producer Shadowman who had Embraced him, Vidal's tone shifted. He spoke at length about his hatred for the man, how Shadowman had shown nothing but disrespect to him and his court, time and again, and that his hospitality had run dry. He offered Elvys a deal: lure Shadowman out into the open so that Donovan could destroy him, and Elvys would escape his own death sentence. Elvys agreed.

His first act as a free vampire was to attend the Conqueror Worm concert, held every Sunday and Wednesday at The Venue, a show he had never once missed. He remembered the way Eleanor had looked at him the night before, when he had choked her, and held onto a fragile hope that she might remember him still.

In the venue bathroom, he found a man doing cocaine and drank from him, riding the borrowed rush through the rest of the show. When it ended and the crowd began to thin, he called out into the room. Not Eleanor, the name she went by now, but the name she had before: "Virginia."

She turned. Recognition spread slowly across her face. "John?"

Joey heard it too, and moved to start a fight. Elvys hit him once, hard, and pulled Eleanor by the hand through the crowd and out of the bar. But once they had put enough distance between themselves and the furious Joey, she stopped running. She looked at him with something like sorrow and said quietly: "She really loved you, you know. Virginia did."

But Virginia had died the day he disappeared: Eleanor told him she was something new now, and she was not Virginia anymore. Before Elvys could press her further, Joey burst through the exit behind them, threatening to kill him for touching his woman. Eleanor stepped between them. She told Joey this was just an old friend, asked him to go back inside, and then turned to Elvys one last time with a quiet farewell before following Joey through the door.

## **Chapter 10: Out of Control**
Amélie had barely returned from Cimitiere's hounfour when she heard the front bell of her shop ring and footsteps pound up the stairs. She was not prepared for Caroline, who appeared in the doorway covered in blood but otherwise unharmed. The young lupine crossed the room and threw her arms around her. "It happened again," she said.

She kept apologizing, but Amélie needed to know what had happened. A handoff gone wrong, she said. She had been shot, she had transformed, but then one of the others, she said, it was one of them. A vampire. She watched him take a bullet and stand up like it was nothing. He had seen her, and now she was certain he was coming for her.

Amélie told her to go in the basement and stayed at the landing, eyes fixed on the front door below. She hoped Caroline had not been followed. If she had, she had no idea what she was going to do.
