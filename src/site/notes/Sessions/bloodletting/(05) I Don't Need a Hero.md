# Summary

## **Chapter 20: An Endless Circle Over Time**
Monday, January 8th.

As tombs tend to be, and it appeared even Choctaw ones were no different, the marked spot on Baron Cimetiere's map was a trap. By entering the cavernous hole they found in the middle of Bayou St. John, the Baron, Lidia, and Amélie Telfort seemed to have been cursed. The hounds of the bayou were right about to pounce and maul them, and a swarm of insects made their escape even less likely.

Lidia tried to run away but was quickly caught by the hounds. Amélie tried to help, but she was attacked by an alligator herself, and it was the Baron who saved them both: he shouted at them to leave him behind, understanding that fighting off these enthralled beasts would be easier without having to worry about protecting his kine.

Lidia and Amélie ran out of the bayou and waited by the car. It took half an hour for the Baron to emerge, mangled but still unliving. On the drive back, the herbalist attempted to ask the Baron, once more, what it was they had found exactly, but the Baron remained elusive. He thanked her for joining them, but kept his concerns to himself: he hadn't known what he would find there, he admitted, but what they found, he said, was much worse than he could have possibly imagined.

## **Chapter 21: The Ways of Love Have Been Abused**
Wendy was sick, but Reggie had taken a terrible risk to save her and wasn't going to give up on her. Andrew seemed to think this resembled a curse, specifically a Crùac one, and Savoy suggested he check in with Doc Xola in the Tremé.

The "clinic" was located in the back of a butcher's shop. Walking in, he was greeted by jars containing all sorts of strange organs, and the place was grimy and shoddily kept, too unsanitary for a butcher, let alone a doctor.

Xola was an immense vampire with a bald head and a long beard. He cycled through the obvious diagnostics first: perhaps Wendy was just having a rough Embrace, and her strange physical alterations were caused by the curse of the Nosferatu. But after Reggie confirmed he was her sire and explained what had happened to her minutes before her Embrace, Xola seemed perplexed. All he could confirm, for now, was that Wendy must have been cursed, and that whoever had done this must possess ritualistic knowledge more advanced than any Acolyte in the city, or must be an ancient Elder.

Reggie was about to leave Wendy in the doctor's care when, getting into his car, he heard the whirring of an electric saw. He rushed back in and had to stop Xola from decapitating her. The doctor argued that Wendy's curse was clearly infectious, and that destroying her was the best way to avoid it spreading. Reggie, however, was able to negotiate: he asked that Xola keep her in one of the meat freezers for a day. He was going to find a solution, he promised, he just needed a day.

Xola agreed, and Reggie was off to see Savoy, the only man he thought could help.

## **Chapter 22: Happy Music in My Soul**
From what he understood, Shadowman was a regular at Leon's, the city's hottest blues bar, until Pearl Chastain quit performing, at which point he stopped coming. From what Sundown had told him, Chastain was forced to end her fifty-year career when her youthful face threatened the Masquerade, and Vidal demanded she retire.

Elvys went to Perdido House to meet with the Prince and explained what he had learned. He then made his suggestion: what if they asked Chastain to perform a special anniversary show at Leon's? This would certainly draw Shadowman out.

The Prince disliked the idea, however. It was too soon after he had asked Chastain to retire, and going back on his word so quickly would make him look disorganized and unconfident. But Elvys pressed that this would certainly work, and would finally help rid him of Shadowman, whom he vehemently despised.

After negotiations, the Prince accepted, but warned Elvys that he was asking a great favor of him, and that for his sake, Shadowman had better be at this show.

After his audience with the Prince, Elvys felt there was one last thing he wanted to try that night. Since being discovered, he had yet to return to the recording studio where Shadowman had Embraced him, and hoped to find some leads on his whereabouts there.

Unfortunately, Studio 504 only confirmed what he already knew, that Shadowman now managed Conqueror Worm. Still, Elvys was able to find tapes of his unreleased album, recorded here two years ago, as well as a vintage white electric guitar, which he also took.

## **Chapter 23: I Don't Need a Soldier**
Andrew was told that searching Meadow's former apartment would be fruitless, as Vidal's Sheriff had already turned it over, and the Prince kept constant eyes on Meadow's known points of operation.

The Prince was right, mostly. While Andrew couldn't find anything else of interest there, he did detect a peculiar scent through his heightened senses, only herbs, and a blend he couldn't quite place. He guessed that having no positive relationships with the Circle of the Crone, even if the Prince had noticed this smell, he wouldn't have the connections to anyone who could identify their purpose.

For this reason, Andrew visited the Telfort Herbarium, a place he had yet to have reason to visit. He was able to accurately describe the smells for its owner Amélie to identify, and after she reproduced the specific blend, she confirmed that burning these herbs was used by some Acolytes to conceal places they didn't want found.

Ironically, it was thanks to this recognizable scent that Andrew was able to find Meadow's secret spot. Based on what Amélie had told him, and after speaking with a few of her peers who were willing to answer his questions, it was clear that Meadows was being helped by an Acolyte. Another source suggested that someone had been using this specific blend of herbs near St. Louis Cemetery.

A little after midnight, Andrew was at the cemetery when, by coincidence, he caught Meadows leaving one of the mausoleums with a woman he didn't recognize. He hesitated between following them and waiting to investigate the mausoleum, and decided the latter would be more discreet. After waiting for them to leave, however, he found nothing inside, nothing but a now-extinguished bundle of herbs, the ones that had brought him here.

He went back out hoping to find Meadows, and while he miraculously managed to catch up to her, they only exchanged a few words before she outran him. It appeared that even though he had affirmed he meant her no harm, having a reputation as an effective Hound makes it hard for fugitive to trust you.
